ShopPilot AI is a privately developed market research and product planning tool focused on digital downloads, templates and software-based products.

01 / RESEARCH
Marketplace research

Organize permitted public listing information and compare product categories and price ranges.

02 / ANALYZE
Opportunity scoring

Assess production effort, automation potential, competition and evidence quality.

03 / PLAN
Product concepts

Develop and review ideas for downloadable templates and digital tools before launch.

About the application

The application is currently a local prototype used only by its developer. Its purpose is research and analysis, not to provide Etsy account access to other users. Data from third-party services is used only where authorized and in accordance with the applicable API terms.

Privacy and data usage

This static website does not have login, analytics, tracking cookies, or submission forms and does not collect visitors' personal data through the page itself. GitHub Pages, as the hosting provider, may process technical connection information according to its own policies. The separate local ShopPilot AI prototype is not publicly accessible through this website.

Independent project

ShopPilot AI is an independent personal software project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Etsy or any other marketplace.
